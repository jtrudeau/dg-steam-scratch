Our Sustainable Business Idea

We are a mobile clinic that is trying to solve the crisis of no access to proper care in Québec. We earn profit through the government.

Our Scratch Project Idea

Two ideas: (1) You control a little truck that goes to people's houses to heal them, then go to the government with a receipt for proof and get money. (2) You go to houses and heal those who need it, then get little stars that upgrade your truck and let you hire new doctors.
