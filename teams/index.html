Chic

Showcasing

We are solving poverty and helping the planet by keeping extra fabric out of the landfill. We sell clothes, and with the donations we get we make blankets for homeless and vulnerable people. We also hire teenagers over 15 who have a hard time finding work, like those in adoption centers or living with a disability, to sew and pick up fabric from factories. We are even helping with bullying, because young people get bullied for how they dress, and that is why Chic is here to help.

Stepping Stone

Showcasing

We assure that the young adults that have aged out of foster care can have an easy reinsertion into society by giving them housing, a job at the Stepping Stone hotel, and an education. We're also helping the planet by having hand/homemade cleaning products (provided at the Stepping Stone hotel), locally sourced produce coming from the hotel's rooftop garden that will be served to the people staying overnight at the hotel and to the young adults working there, and energy efficient light bulbs placed around the hotel and in the building the young adults will be living in.
