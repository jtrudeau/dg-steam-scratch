Screen Capture for Your Presentation

At the sustainability fair, your main presentation is your business idea. Your Scratch game is supporting — it shows what you built.

Today: record a 1–2 minute screen capture of someone playing your game and explaining what is on screen. You will embed this video in your presentation later.

You are not presenting your business to the class today.

Step 1: Plan Your Play-Through (15 min)

Open your Scratch project. Play it.

Pick one short path (about 1–2 minutes). You do not need to show the whole game.

Write 3 to 5 steps:

StepWhat you click or seeWhat you say (one sentence)
1
2
3
4
5

Example sentences:

"I click Start."

"Here you choose which material to use."

"The score goes up when you pick the sustainable option."

Keep it simple. Say what is on the screen.

Step 2: Practice Once (10 min)

One teammate plays and talks at the same time.

  • Use a timer. Stop at 2 minutes.
  • If something does not work, skip it in the recording.
  • Change any sentence that sounds confusing.

Step 3: Record With Mr. Joel (when ready)

Sign up when your team is ready.

Before recording

  • Scratch open and working (green flag tested)
  • Someone knows the steps

Recording

  • Screen capture only — your game on screen + your voice
  • 1–2 minutes
  • Small mistakes are ok. Keep going.

After

  • Save the file / link in your team Google Doc so you can add it to your presentation

Teams waiting: keep practicing quietly.

Remember

  • Business idea = your main presentation
  • Scratch recording = short proof of what you built
  • Show what works. Skip what does not.
Facilitator Notes

Teacher Goal

Each team gets a short screen recording they can drop into their sustainability fair presentation. The main presentation stays about the business idea.

Artifacts

  • 1–2 minute screen recording

Flow

  1. Plan the play-through (15 min)

    Play the game. Pick 3–5 clicks or scenes that fit in 1–2 minutes. Write one short sentence per step.

  2. Practice once (10 min)

    One person plays and talks. Time it. Fix anything that breaks or takes too long.

  3. Screen capture with Mr. Joel (35 min)

    Sign up when ready. Record screen + voice only. 1–2 minutes max.

Teacher Moves

  • Clarify up front: the fair presentation is about their business. Scratch is proof they built something — today's job is only the recording.
  • One path, 90 seconds is fine. Skip unfinished parts.
  • Queue on the board. Presenter plays and narrates; teammate can hold the script.
  • Glitches: skip and keep going.
