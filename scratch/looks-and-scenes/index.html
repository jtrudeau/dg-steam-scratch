Looks + Scenes

lookscostumesbackdrops

Use costumes, backdrops, and visual effects to tell a story.

Milestones

  1. Costumes: Switch costumes to animate a sprite.
  2. Backdrops: Change scenes to show progress or location.
  3. Effects: Use simple effects sparingly for emphasis.

Guide

Goal

Create visual changes that help the audience follow the action. Looks are not decoration only; they communicate state, emotion, progress, and scene changes.

What to use

  • Costumes: animate a sprite or show a different state.
  • Backdrops: move between places or screens.
  • Say/think: give short instructions or feedback.
  • Show/hide: control what the audience should focus on.
  • Effects: use sparingly for special moments.

Try it

Switch backdrops after a short wait.

when green flag clicked
switch backdrop to [city v]
wait (1) seconds
switch backdrop to [space v]

Animate a sprite

Put this on a sprite with at least two costumes.

when green flag clicked
forever
  next costume
  wait (0.2) seconds
end

If the animation is too fast, increase the wait. If it is too slow, decrease the wait.

Make a start and win screen

Put this on the Stage.

when green flag clicked
switch backdrop to [Start v]

when I receive [win v]
switch backdrop to [Win v]

Put this on a guide sprite.

when green flag clicked
say [Use arrow keys. Collect 5 points.] for (4) seconds

when I receive [win v]
say [You win!] for (2) seconds

Design rule

The player should always know:

  • What is happening now.
  • What they can do next.
  • Whether they succeeded, missed, or need to retry.

Checkpoint

You can change scenes, animate a sprite, and use short messages without breaking the rest of the scripts.
